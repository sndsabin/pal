What's inside

Everything time-related, in your tray

Clocks

See where everyone is, at a glance

Add the cities that matter and pal shows each one's local time, date and current weather, plus exactly how far ahead or behind it is from home.

Weather

Weather for every place you track

A detailed view of your home city with the current temperature, feels-like reading and a scrollable hourly forecast, followed by conditions for all your other locations.

Time Converter

Plan the call without the mental math

Pick a date, a time and a city, and instantly see what time it will be in your other locations. Daylight saving changes are handled for you.

Timer

A quick timer, right where you are

Start a countdown without opening another app. The progress ring and the exact time the alarm will ring keep you on track.

How to run

  1. 1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security .
  2. 2. Scroll down to the Security section.
  3. 3. Find the notification for pal and click Open Anyway.
  4. 4. Enter your password or use Touch ID to confirm.
